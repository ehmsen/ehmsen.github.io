## Block 3 — Pace held, threshold in (current)

**2026-11-02 → 11-29 · 4 weeks · 13.0 h/week of booked time on average**

**What I'm testing:** if I swim three to four times a week, with one continuous set each Saturday that I start at an even pace and read from the pace clock, my pace holds late in a long swim. The test is the continuous 2,400 m on Saturday 14 November: my last 400 m must be no more than 4 s per 100 m slower than my first 400 m. In October my 400 m splits went from 2:00 to 2:20. The average must also be 2:11/100 m or faster.

Bike threshold work starts this block: one threshold ride a week. New things come in one week at a time: week 1 the pool swim and the first threshold ride, week 2 the first brick, week 3 a third hard session, week 4 the bike test.

Bike targets are trainer watts from my 234 W test. Run targets are Stryd power from my 302 W threshold, or a fixed pace where the pace is what I measure. Swims are in metres.
